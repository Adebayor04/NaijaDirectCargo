Prohibited & restricted items

For safety and customs compliance, some items can't be shipped at all, and others need checking with us first before you book a collection.

We cannot accept

  • Firearms, ammunition, explosives, fireworks or prohibited weapons
  • Illegal drugs, narcotics or controlled substances
  • Counterfeit, stolen or otherwise illegal goods
  • Cash, banknotes and negotiable financial instruments
  • Highly flammable, explosive, corrosive, toxic or hazardous chemicals
  • Petrol, diesel, gas cylinders and other fuels
  • Toxic or nuclear waste
  • Human remains
  • Live animals
  • Certain prohibited meat, poultry, food and agricultural products
  • Any goods prohibited by UK export regulations or Nigerian import regulations

⚠️ Check with us before shipping

  • Second-hand clothing
  • Perfumes and cosmetics
  • Liquids
  • Medicines and medical products
  • Food and drinks
  • Alcohol
  • Tobacco products
  • Batteries and battery-powered items
  • Laptops, mobile phones and other electronics
  • Vehicle parts
  • Engines and machinery
  • Refrigerators and freezers
  • Paints, aerosols and certain chemicals
  • Plants, seeds and agricultural products
  • High-value goods

Important

All shipment contents must be accurately declared. Please do not conceal prohibited or restricted items inside boxes, bags, furniture, appliances or other cargo.

Prohibited or incorrectly declared goods may be refused, removed, detained, seized or returned, and the customer may be responsible for any resulting costs.

Import and customs requirements can change. If you're unsure about an item, please contact us before sending it.

Questions we get a lot

How long does shipping take?

Sea freight from the UK to Lagos typically takes several weeks door-to-port, depending on the sailing schedule and how quickly a container fills — we'll give you a realistic window when you book.

How do I pack a barrel?

Pack heavier items at the bottom, wrap breakables well, and leave a little give at the top. We'll send you a short packing guide once you book a collection.

What can't I send?

We can't ship prohibited or restricted goods — flammables, weapons, illegal items, and certain electronics or liquids. Ask us if you're unsure about a specific item.

How do I track my shipment?

You'll get a tracking code as soon as your cargo leaves our UK warehouse, and we'll update you as it moves through to Lagos.

How do I pay?

We'll confirm the price and payment options with you directly once we know what you're sending and where it's going.

Do you deliver outside Lagos?

Yes — collection from our Lagos office is standard, and we can arrange onward delivery to select other states. Ask us when you book.
